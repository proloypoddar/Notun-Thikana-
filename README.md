## 🏠 Notun Thikana

**Notun Thikana** is a community-powered web platform built to help people — especially newcomers — settle into city life more easily. From finding houses to discovering local events, posting anonymously, and connecting with others, the platform brings together essential lifestyle services in one place.

The goal is to create a digital space where people can share, explore, and engage with their city through community forums, events, and helpful tools.
